    Times are computed for the chosen place (drik ganitam, Lahiri ayanamsa) and checked against drikpanchang.com. Each day carries the tithi running at its sunrise. Vrata days use simple rules; families who follow a matha panchangam may keep Ekadasi a day apart.

    Sandhyavandanam follows the Krishna Yajurveda, Apastamba order common in Telugu Smarta homes. On this page its Sanskrit is in Roman letters with pronunciation marks, without svaras. It is a reading aid: learn the mantras and their svaras from your guru. Kshetra wordings are drafted from common usage; confirm with your purohit.

    In Tamil, mantras and the sankalpam are shown in English letters for now; a version in Tamil script is being prepared and checked.

    The code is open for anyone to use and build on: github.com/usyvsaditya-del/telugu-sankalpam
